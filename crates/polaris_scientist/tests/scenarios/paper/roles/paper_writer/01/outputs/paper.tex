\begin{abstract}
We study a seed circle-packing program and a pair-swap step that raises its
score from 1.0 to 2.0.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
Circle packing asks how large a sum of radii fits into a unit
square~\cite{Graham1996,Collins2003}.
\section{Related Work}
Dense packings have been studied for decades~\cite{Szabo2007,Lubachevsky1997};
we use simple local search~\cite{Kirkpatrick1983}.
\section{Method}
The retained program swaps pairs of circles and keeps a swap when the score
rises.
\section{Results}
The best retained program scores 2.0, against 1.0 for the seed.
